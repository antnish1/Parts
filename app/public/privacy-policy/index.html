Privacy Policy

Frontier Commercial Vehicles Pvt. Ltd. · Parts Connect Portal

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Frontier Commercial Vehicles Pvt. Ltd. ("Frontier", "we", "our") handles information when authorized personnel use Parts Connect Portal, including the Android application and its related web portal (collectively, the "Service"). The Service supports internal parts and operational workflows and is not intended for general consumer account registration.

1. Information we process

Depending on your role and the features you use, the Service may process:

  • Account and employee information: user ID, name, business contact details, assigned branch, role, authentication information and account status.
  • Operational records: parts orders, line items, approval and issuance history, dispatch/docket details, inventory and location records, comments, attachments, customer or machine references, credit-dispatch and TA/DA information, and audit/activity history.
  • Device and notification information: push notification tokens, device/platform identifiers needed for notification delivery, notification status, diagnostic information, and basic usage or error information.
  • Information you choose to provide: scanned barcodes, documents, images or files submitted for authorized business workflows.

Some operational documents may contain customer or business-contact information. Authorized users should upload only information necessary for their assigned work.

2. Why we use this information

  • Authenticate employees and enforce branch- and role-based access.
  • Process parts requests, approvals, dispatch, delivery, installations, credit and operational records.
  • Maintain histories, reports, accountability, audit trails and operational security.
  • Send relevant service alerts and push notifications where notification permission is granted.
  • Investigate errors, troubleshoot and improve the reliability and safety of the Service.

3. Permissions and device access

The Android application may request camera access to scan part or docket codes; access to selected images or documents to attach business records; and notification permission to deliver work-related alerts. Access is requested in connection with supported features and may be controlled through Android settings. We do not use these permissions for advertising.

4. Service providers and disclosures

We use technology providers to operate the Service, including Supabase for backend data and authentication, Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging for mobile notification delivery, and infrastructure or hosting providers for application availability. These services may process information necessary to perform the relevant functions. Authorized internal personnel may access operational information according to assigned responsibilities. We may disclose information where required by applicable law or for legitimate security and compliance purposes.

We do not sell employee or customer personal information and do not use Service data to target third-party advertisements.

5. Security

We use access controls, authenticated accounts, role-based permissions and encrypted transport where supported by our infrastructure. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their credentials and promptly report unauthorized access to their designated company administrator.

6. Retention and deletion

Account information, operational records, logs and attachments are retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant business workflow, recordkeeping, auditing, dispute resolution or legal obligations. Retention depends on the record type and applicable company requirements. Where appropriate, records may be corrected, restricted, de-identified or deleted. Some transaction and audit records may need to be preserved even after employee access ends.

7. Your privacy requests

Authorized users may request access to or correction of their information, report inaccurate records, or request deletion of eligible personal information through their company administrator or the support channel provided within their workplace. Requests are reviewed in light of authorization, business recordkeeping and applicable law.

Account deletion: As this is a managed employee system, users should contact their company's designated Parts Connect administrator to request account deactivation and review of personal-data deletion. Deactivation may not remove business transactions that must be retained.

8. Children

The Service is intended for authorized business personnel, not children, and is not designed to collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy to reflect product, legal or operational changes. An updated effective date will appear on this page. Material changes may also be communicated through appropriate company channels.

10. Contact

For privacy enquiries, requests or complaints regarding Parts Connect Portal, contact the Parts Connect administrator or the designated IT/privacy contact at Frontier Commercial Vehicles Pvt. Ltd. through your established company support channel. If you are not an employee, ask the Frontier representative who supplied your information to route your enquiry to the responsible administrator.
